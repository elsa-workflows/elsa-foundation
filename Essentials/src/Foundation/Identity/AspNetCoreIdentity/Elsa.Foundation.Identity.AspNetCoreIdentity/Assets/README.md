# Bundled login artwork

Ember (warm coast/path), Tidal (cool coast), Daybreak (soft sunrise) and Canopy (green woodland)
were generated for the Studio design exploration with OpenAI image generation and approved by the
product owner in the design session. These optimized JPEG derivatives ship as local decorative assets.
They require no third-party photo/CDN account. Integrators may replace them through LoginPageOptions.
The source originals and prompts remain in the task's design artifacts; they are not required to build.

Sketchbook adds the owner-approved fine-line capability drawing and graphite/watercolor rendition of the official Elsa mark, generated with the built-in image tool for the 1 November 2026 product brochure. They are optimized local derivatives (`sketchbook.jpg`, `sketchbook-logo.png`) and use the same replacement options. The Studio shell has its own bundled copy of the small default mark so Identity and Studio remain independently deployable.
