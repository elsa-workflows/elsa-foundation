# Provisional external entity ingress assessment

Research snapshot: 6 November 2026, 00:50 UTC. This records current candidates and a behavioral review target; it does not approve a shared ABI or resume any adjacent program.

**Decision: common direction, no agreed or qualified common ingress yet.** Ordinary embedded Event waits/resume exist, but neither published candidate supplies an admitted, implemented external entity-event route satisfying spec 1219 FR-033. Continue metadata/read/operation work independently; do not create a third source representation or make delivery depend on the complete trigger, messaging or StateMachine products.

**Minimum contract to take to the Runtime owners:** admit a normalized external Event without requiring a standalone trigger resource. Runtime derives trusted scope/source authority, fixes the ordinary embedded-start and exact pre-existing-wait cohort before effects, durably retains that source/cohort and independent recipient responsibility, and returns a stable acceptance/duplicate/conflict or explicit no-match/refusal result. Retries recover the original cohort and exact wait generations rather than discovering later waits; source/target proof governs completion, restart and retention. The owners must choose its concrete entry point, physical representation, authority rules and horizon. This is a behavioral review target, not an adopted CLR/wire ABI.

**FR-033 has a concrete missing route.** Current `Event` can suspend mid-flow, and [`StimulusRouter.RouteAsync`](https://github.com/elsa-workflows/elsa-foundation/blob/e0ba87ecfc3b1e774c509ed274b19cd3f34a1124/Essentials/src/Workflows/Runtime/Elsa.Workflows.Runtime/Services/Triggers/StimulusRouter.cs#L87) snapshots existing waits before starting new executions. That live route does not retain an accepted external cohort. The durable [saved route](https://github.com/elsa-workflows/elsa-foundation/blob/e0ba87ecfc3b1e774c509ed274b19cd3f34a1124/Essentials/src/Workflows/Runtime/Elsa.Workflows.Runtime/Services/Triggers/StimulusRouter.Saved.cs#L9) requires a canonical workflow outbox claim; [selection](https://github.com/elsa-workflows/elsa-foundation/blob/e0ba87ecfc3b1e774c509ed274b19cd3f34a1124/Essentials/src/Workflows/Runtime/Elsa.Workflows.Runtime/Services/Triggers/StimulusDeliveryServices.cs#L46) reads the real publisher execution. A fabricated publisher or captured DTO is not an external ingress solution.

#3912's proposed public port requires `TriggerId`; its standalone branch explicitly lacks the existing exact-wait `RecipientSnapshot`, and its accepted recipients are subscription root starts. A concrete case—one workflow already waiting for Claim Approved, no root-start subscription—has no specified accepted embedded-wait route; zero admitted subscriptions refuses. [Public port](https://github.com/elsa-workflows/elsa-foundation/blob/f064e47b7aa11f26f70309f4b002c7db5dac4796/specs/1217-standalone-triggers/contracts/runtime.md#L122), [source arms](https://github.com/elsa-workflows/elsa-foundation/blob/f064e47b7aa11f26f70309f4b002c7db5dac4796/specs/1217-standalone-triggers/contracts/runtime.md#L49).

C2 explicitly proposes external exact starts/waits and original-winner replay in its [mapping 28–38](https://github.com/elsa-workflows/elsa-foundation/blob/d9678af6f859cefa5f307f22f2a806857bf94a0e/specs/1210-state-machine/m0-runtime-mapping.md#L28). This is useful owned implementation work to reconcile, not an available FR-033 capability.

**Exact agreement needed before entity-event implementation:**

1. Runtime source/persistence owners of #3912/#3904 and retained C2/#3820 must review one concrete canonical source/claim/completion/retention mapping, preserving real native outbox provenance and legacy wire. Their recorded agreement must identify which physical/public representation is authoritative; the component SPI alone is not that agreement.
2. Runtime Event routing/resumption and entity integration owners must settle the producer entry point for normal embedded starts **and** waits/resume without requiring a standalone trigger resource. A standalone trigger may be an optional adapter above that route, not a prerequisite. Cover original embedded waits, starts, empty selections and legacy ambiguity, with cohort fixation before effects and exact wait generations. Do not route entity Events through Message's competing/late-buffered policy merely to obtain a wait API.
3. Agree the event profile's identity/dedup horizon, fixed-recipient point, consumed/later-wait protection, restart/partial-fanout recovery and source-to-target proof. FR-033 requires two original existing executions to resume, foreign source/tenant isolation, and no duplicate acquisition by later waits within the horizon; start-only evidence cannot pass.
4. Agree trusted producer/workload context, resource grant, accepted-grant revocation/cancellation and current target policy responsibilities. Keep interactive users, autonomous resources, source account permissions and retained Runtime acceptance authority distinct. The entity adapter supplies authorized source observation, stable source/event/reference normalization, declared commit/capability evidence and bounded permitted payload; Runtime selects recipients and owns acceptance, exact resume authority and recovery. No credentials, tenant switches or recipient grants come from event data.

These are bounded shared-kernel/consumer agreements and corresponding provider/restart witnesses. They do not require finishing HTTP/Timer management UI, durable Message buffering, StateMachine semantics or other paused products. No PR/source was changed, no producer was resumed, no build/host/test ran, and no external message was sent. Only temporary research captures and this requested report were written.
