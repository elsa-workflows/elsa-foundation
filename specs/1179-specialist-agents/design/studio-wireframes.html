Execution preview

Writer → reviewer. A rejected review sends article and comments to the next writer. Three rounds includes the initial attempt: at most two rewrites, six model calls.

The approved predicate completes the team. Exhaustion takes an explicit fault/manual-review path.

One accepted team result continues its native or BPMN parent. Human content approval is a separate process step.
