## Bite-proofs

[`bite-proofs.json`](bite-proofs.json) has 32 entries, and all bite. Two, recorded at `b1d81e2e3` (review round 3), cover the driver's maximum and a 540 reply (`basic.get` on a stream queue) being a rejection. Six, recorded at `9411620cf` (review round 2), cover the writer turning a blank body and a multi-line body that is not JSON into a rejected line, the execution acknowledging at all, the driver's drain time bound and its taking at least one message, and an acknowledgement-timeout 406 being transient; together with round 1's entries they supersede four entries recorded at `9b4c621db` (`writer-line-breaks`, `writer-blank`, `execution-acks` and `driver-first-too-large`), whose code the round-1 streaming rewrite (`7852daed4`) replaced; the first-message bound the last of them covered moved from the driver to the preparer, where round 1's `preparer-first-too-large` covers it. Seven, recorded at `7852daed4` (review round 1), cover the execution disposing the receipt and acknowledging with the caller's token, the preparer choosing transient or rejected, failing a first message over the bound, and checking the bound on each written line, and the writer dropping a byte order mark and turning line breaks into spaces. The other 17, recorded at `9b4c621db`, cover the compiler's queue and maximum rules; the worker's queue and maximum re-checks; the binding's allowed queue, its need for the consumer and the queues in its handle; the snapshot writer's line-break and blank-body handling; the per-attempt snapshot identity; the preparer requeuing on failure; the execution acknowledging at all and only after the record is applied or replayed; the JSON Lines processing view; and, on the real broker, the driver's manual acknowledgement, its multiple acknowledgement and its first-message bound; four of them are the superseded entries above.

**Not bite-proven or not tested:**
- **The crash window after the record and before the acknowledgement.** Its outcome, the broker redelivering the messages to a later run, follows from the broker's own semantics, which the container suite proves for an unacknowledged receipt; no test kills a worker inside that window.
- **A worker process killed before the record.** Equivalent to a receipt closed unacknowledged, which the container suite proves on the broker and the journeys prove through the runtime; no test kills a worker process holding a real broker connection.
- **Guards tested but not bite-proven:** the driver rejecting a message that does not fit back to the queue (closing the channel would requeue it as well); the batch builder's, record codec's and runtime API's acceptance of a `RabbitMq` source pin; the acknowledgement failure being logged rather than failing the run; and the Studio controls.
- **Untested:** a failure writing the snapshot itself being reported as a snapshot write failure rather than classified as a broker failure; more than one consumer being registered.
- **Defensive re-checks, tested through the paths that use them but not bite-proven:** the preparer's re-checks of a RabbitMQ source pin (no digest, a hashed handle) and of the source's fields, queue and maximum; the executable reader's field-count bound and JSON pointer validation for the source; the artifact codec refusing null field entries; the compiler's size budget for the source; and the connector bindings' ambiguity check for a source alias.

## Actual Workbench journey

[`rabbitmq_source_workbench_journey.py`](rabbitmq_source_workbench_journey.py) starts its own RabbitMQ 4.1 container and its own Workbench on ephemeral loopback ports and publishes five messages, one not JSON. A RabbitMQ → JSON Lines definition with a maximum of three messages runs twice from native Flowcharts:
- **first:** takes three messages, delivers Ada and Bram and routes the invalid body to errors, through the BusinessError port; two messages stay queued and none is left unacknowledged;
- **second:** takes Cleo and Dee through the Done port; the queue is then empty with nothing unacknowledged.

All eight checks pass. The summary is [`workbench-journey-summary.json`](workbench-journey-summary.json), recorded at `b1d81e2e3`, with only this README modified. Earlier runs at `d747a18c9`, `7852daed4` and `9411620cf` passed the same checks. Queue counts are read live with `rabbitmqctl`; the management API's statistics lag by seconds.

## Limits

These are stated in the ETL README:
- **At least once:** messages whose acknowledgement does not reach the broker are read again by a later run; records carry no message id.
- **Finite:** a run drains what is queued, up to its maximum; a continuous source is deferred.
- **Two runs reading one queue** take different messages.
