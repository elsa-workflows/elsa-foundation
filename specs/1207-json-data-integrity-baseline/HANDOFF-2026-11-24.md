Publishing binary and produced a false current-source failure. Source qualification was blocked first by reference
selection, then by warnings appended to SDK JSON output. Large chained runs stopped at the first such failure, leaving
later checks unexecuted. These are execution/fixture problems, not evidence that every failure required another product
redesign. Repeating broad builds or source-only reviews alone will not resolve the remaining gate evidence.
