## Reader integrity and limits

The reader opens a closed coherent SQLite snapshot as immutable/query-only and verifies its complete expected hash before and after. Five controls accept the actual capture and refuse a wrong expected hash, a foreign captured pin, an owed orphan row hidden behind a clean canonical source and an acquired dispatch capability declaration. No snapshot changes or raw exception details leak to logs. A compiled mutation removes only the enumerated physical-source identity guard; the orphan is then incorrectly accepted and the control fails. Restoring the exact source makes all controls pass again. This mutation qualifies the evidence reader, rather than changing runtime semantics.

Final actual API results are **old host 2/2** and **upgraded host 2/2**, with checked capture and all three resulting stores passing. The extra cold portability control also passes 2/2 with matching normalized outcomes. Original eight legacy captures, historical receipts and frozen authoring stay unchanged. Foundation's prior EF 1,710/class32 results remain source-bound inherited observations; this increment adds scripts and the read-only tool without changing production runtime source. Three independent bounded component audits are clean; full-PR rounds remain zero, bound six.

Earlier attempts remain excluded: a high-load old build lost MSBuild workers and passed with two workers/isolated compiler processes; an archive build inherited the enclosing checkout's version suffix and was discarded before qualified capture; the first API parking fixture assumed an `items` wrapper instead of the old bare array; a runtime-only capture paired with a newly seeded design catalog was correctly refused by the template source-reference guard. A coherent private catalog/runtime copy established the cause, then the final capture separated the complete Design/Publishing store from identity at creation. The final bundle requires both stores; no reference guard was weakened. The reader's first blocking-incident assertion also incorrectly required a null resolution instead of requiring the actual non-absorbing FaultWorkflow outcome; the final assertion preserves the original fault and blocking state. An initial guard mutation escaped because the private negative fixture left a sidecar, causing an earlier closure refusal. Final negative variants are explicitly committed and closed, then copied through SQLite backup to fresh sidecar-free snapshots; integrity, unchanged canonical source and absence of sidecars are positively required before invoking the reader. The final guard mutation therefore reaches the intended canonical-reload gap and incorrectly accepts that closed orphan.

This proves two actual legacy published-call recoveries in Balanced mode, including their ordinary Faulted-result policy. It does not establish the complete new opted-in control bridge on Workbench, current authoring/compiler behavior for that full matrix, final real Studio, production acceptance, arbitrary operator alteration, distributed-provider recovery or running-binary rollback. T045 remains unchecked until its remaining actual-host evidence exists. T029, T032–T038, separate ADR0063 and independent #2898/#2907/program acceptance stay open; Orleans/task-runner are deferred.
