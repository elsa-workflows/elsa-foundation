Program: [Modular Hosting Upstream Delivery](../../program-goals/modular-hosting-upstream-delivery.md), issue [#2500](https://github.com/elsa-workflows/elsa-foundation/issues/2500). These decisions constrain implementation. M3's design is selected; its implementation and safety proof remain open below.

## M3 selected design and remaining safety gate

**Status: design selected; implementation and safety proof remain open.** Root's 2026-10-09 engineering selection supersedes the former owner-answer hold. Nuplane Spec 030 is committed/pushed on branch `108-safe-package-store-pruning` at `85a96e7611c27d5859b513e38f69941df7051c23`; it is requirements-only, and planning is underway. No product code or package store changed. This selection does not mark M3 ready.

Before safe execution is accepted, proof must cover multi-state/offline active and LKG closure, every managed path reader and lease-before-read, crash recovery, cross-process coordination, aliases/path replacement, actual Windows behavior, fresh-plan races, and real deletion/partial outcomes in owned test roots. Report-only or always-refused behavior does not satisfy the selected end-to-end objective; the native #2509 dependency on Nuplane #108 remains intentional. Foundation #2362 owns assembly unloading and Foundation #2354 owns host-composition extraction; this program must not duplicate either.

### Historical owner-answer checkpoint — superseded 2026-10-09

The earlier spike found that install-root and state-file identity are independent, and its report required an owner choice between authoritative binding and complete multi-state protection. Root has selected multi-state physical-root coordination. The historical spike evidence and its finding that upgraded binaries cannot detect nonparticipating readers remain valid; only the “owner choice pending” status is superseded.
