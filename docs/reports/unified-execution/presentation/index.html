    Preserve: semantic ownership

    Flowchart joins stay in Flowchart. Sequence ordering stays in Sequence. BPMN tokens and event races stay in BPMN. The kernel persists decisions; it does not invent them.

    Revise: cancellation and outcomes

    The experimental completion flag and work cancellation do not express a complete model lifecycle. A durable cancellation input and model-owned terminal outcome need explicit contracts.

    Clarify: actor and parallelism boundaries

    Keep one serialized coordinator per execution. Parallel workers perform independently claimed work. ADR 0031's existing concurrency ceiling needs an explicit amendment for this distinction.

    Preserve: one persistence and membership authority

    Actor providers must not create a competing durable truth or liveness registry. Production first-party persistence remains EF under ADR 0073; the experiments do not change that policy.
