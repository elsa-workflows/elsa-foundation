# EF Core persistence repository-surface register

Status: closed. Every row below carries a merged PR, the deletion PR #1764, or an owner-authorized
retained/historical disposition. This register is completion evidence for
[#1671](https://github.com/elsa-workflows/elsa-foundation/issues/1671) and is no longer a worklist.

Original snapshot: `main` at `7a952efcf8d53472d7d4e7e3fd7b51d7a808c1c8` (2026-09-12).
Closed against `main` at `a83c41c1c50904435bcd91f4b25ff4bfbd8d3d51`, the merge of
[#1764](https://github.com/elsa-workflows/elsa-foundation/pull/1764), whose parent is
`569903c80c1ded896294efc601a1f5d976194ef5` — the merge of
[#1763](https://github.com/elsa-workflows/elsa-foundation/pull/1763), which flipped the Workbench,
Production and Docker compositions to EF Core by default.

This register covers package and project references, provider configuration, migration/schema
artifacts, Workbench/host wiring, tools, workflows/alerts, solution membership, generated maps,
architecture guards, MongoDB surfaces, and performance infrastructure. Historical ADRs, reports,
specs, and evidence remain truthful historical records unless a row explicitly identifies an active
claim that needs qualification.

`Retire` means retired by owner policy and never means performance passed. No row in this register
records a performance result, and none may: performance measurement was retired by owner decision
under ADR 0073 and [#1668](https://github.com/elsa-workflows/elsa-foundation/issues/1668) (merged as
[#1756](https://github.com/elsa-workflows/elsa-foundation/pull/1756)). Benchmarks are never run and
no performance claim may be made from these surfaces.

Where a table combines replacement/deletion evidence into one PR cell, record all applicable PRs
there explicitly. Default-flip is `N/A` for packages, tools, workflows, alerts, generated artifacts,
transaction questions and historical documents; host/composition rows carry a distinct default-flip
cell. Dependencies are the named owner plus any prerequisite stated in the required-outcome cell.

## Two closing steps — #1763 and #1764

**#1763 (merged, `569903c80`)** flipped the shipped compositions. `shells.json`,
`shells.baseline.json`, `shells.Production.json` and `docker/compose/elsa-workbench.shells.json`
now select the EF module features; every module resolves `ConnectionStrings:Elsa` (SQLite `elsa.db`
locally, the compose PostgreSQL service in Docker) unless a feature overrides its own
`ConnectionString`; migrations run in the CShells `Prepare` phase ahead of shell tasks and seeders.

**#1764 (merged, `a83c41c1c`)** deletes the retired families. 14 source projects, 36 test
projects, `tests/Groundwork/`, `tools/groundwork/` and `e2e-tests/groundwork/` are gone; the seven
`Groundwork.*` pins, `MongoDB.Driver` and `Testcontainers.MongoDb` are out of
`Directory.Packages.props`; the Groundwork feed and `MongoDB.*` mapping are out of `NuGet.config`;
`src/Elsa/Directory.Build.targets` (which escalated Groundwork's GW0004 analyzer diagnostic) is
deleted; `Elsa.Server.slnx` loses 50 projects and 44 folders; the two Groundwork `.slnf` files are
replaced by one regenerated `Elsa.Server.Persistence.Integration.slnf`; the Groundwork-specific
architecture guards are replaced by `RetiredPersistenceFamilyGuardTests`; and the
`groundwork-v2-native-provider-matrix` CI job is replaced by `ef-container-suites`.

Rows are attributed to whichever of the two actually did the work. Several rows this register
previously marked `Deleted by claude/delete-groundwork` were in fact closed by #1763; they are
corrected below.
